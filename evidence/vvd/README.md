Recordings of these sessions (emulator-console `screenrecord`, 1080p) are the source of the demo video. Timings are from
the Vega Virtual Device on an Apple M-series Mac — no Fire TV hardware was used.

**Earlier session (2026-10-12).** The Mac's screen locked at 22:36 WIB (`host-screen-lock.txt`)
(15:36 UTC; `CGSSessionScreenLockedTime` from `ioreg`). The already-running VVD kept working (both 10-12 logs above are
after the lock); from about 23:13 WIB the dev title's calibration overlay did not draw, and after a VVD restart at
23:27 WIB the VVD never booted again (QEMU "hanging thread" after GPU init, also with `--no-gui` and on a fresh
instance). On 2026-10-13, with the screen unlocked, it booted in about 7 s. On 10-13 the VVD also stopped drawing text
and plain views in the app after about 20 minutes of uptime (icons still drew; it survived an app relaunch); a VVD
restart cleared it. Not yet measured on the device: a dedicated 100-frame decode median.
