How it works

Scan. Mark. Jump. On the TV.

FLIPBOOKv0.0.0-dev · 04 / 10
Everything runs on the TV. Amazon's sample parser hands us the frames; jpeg-js decodes them and we keep only the calibrated score box. Pass 1 samples every eighth frame and bisects where the box differs; pass 2 reads every frame and can only add. The KUI SeekBar draws the ticks and masks thumbnails; our key handler owns fast-forward.
